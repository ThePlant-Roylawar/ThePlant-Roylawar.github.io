# About

The **Roylawar Plant–Microbiome Laboratory** is based in the Department of Botany, S. N. Arts, D.J.M. Commerce & B.N.S. Science College (Autonomous), Sangamner, Maharashtra, India.

Our laboratory investigates beneficial plant-associated microorganisms and develops sustainable biological solutions for improving crop productivity, disease management, and climate resilience.

Our research integrates:

- Plant–Microbe Interactions
- Rhizosphere Microbiology
- Metagenomics
- Plant Pathology
- Nanotechnology
- Biological Control
- Sustainable Agriculture
- Bioinformatics
