# Version 2.1
**Date:** 02 August 2026

## Homepage Redesign

### Hero Section

Decision:
- Replaced the standard Quarto hero with a custom scientific banner designed specifically for the Roy Plant–Microbiome Laboratory.

Reason:
- To immediately communicate the laboratory's research identity through original scientific visuals.

Integrated figures:
- Onion root system
- Pathogen response to nanoparticles
- Microbial network analysis
- TEM nanoparticle characterization

---

### Homepage Structure

Final structure:

1. Hero
2. About the Lab
3. Research Impact
4. Featured Research
5. Research Collaborations
6. Footer

Reason:
- Creates a logical narrative:
  - Who we are
  - What we study
  - Our research impact
  - Our collaborators

---

### Featured Research

Decision:
- Replaced the generic "Research Highlights" section with a more meaningful "Featured Research" section.

Reason:
- Better reflects the laboratory's flagship research areas rather than listing general topics.

Current flagship research:

- Wild Allium Rhizosphere Microbiome
- Nanotechnology for Plant Disease Management
- Plant–Microbiome Interactions

---

### Design Philosophy

The website should:

- look like an international research laboratory website
- use original scientific figures whenever possible
- maintain a consistent green identity
- remain lightweight and fast
- be fully responsive
- prioritize readability over visual clutter
